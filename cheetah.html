Character Profile

Cheetah

Speed Cannot Outrun Fate

Origin

Overview

Karina grew up in a structured home, but distance from her parents and sister left her isolated. Her life changed after finding a crystal on a morning run. Once she acquired its power, she left home and joined the ARC. Her speed, discipline, and instincts quickly made her stand out.

Arc

Character Journey

Karina completed solo missions as a scout and proved herself repeatedly in battle. Her biggest challenge came with a teammate assignment: Bunny. After intense battles and a costly victory, she carried deep guilt over his sacrifice. Later, Isaac recruited her into The Five with Jae, where she started building a chosen family.

Temperament

Personality

Karina is cold and quiet on the surface, shaped by loss and pressure. Bunny's influence helped her trust others, and now she protects her team with intense loyalty. Her actions reveal care even when words do not.

Combat Kit

Abilities

  • Superhuman speed and reflexes enhanced by the Cheetah crystal
  • Dual-knife combat expertise combining precision and agility
  • High-level scouting and infiltration capability
