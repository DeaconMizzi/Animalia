Overview

Karina grew up in a well-put-together home, but her relationship with her family wasn’t the strongest. Difficult to see her parents due to their lack of presence at home and her sister neglecting her, Karina became cold and distant. Her life took a turn upon encountering a crystal on her morning run. After acquiring its power, she made the difficult decision to leave her family and join the ARC. Her exceptional skills and abilities stood out in the organization, particularly in how well her traits aligned with her animal power.

Character Journey

Karina embarked on several solo missions as a scout, excelling at infiltrating and escaping with ease. She also proved herself in battle countless times. Her greatest challenge came when she was assigned a teammate: Bunny. Together, they faced intense battles, ultimately achieving victory, but at the cost of Bunny’s life. Despite Bunny’s self-sacrifice, Karina blames herself for the loss, pushing her to strive harder and protect her comrades. This tragedy marked her evolution as a person and a warrior.

Right as Karina considered isolating herself again, she was approached by Isaac to join The Five, where she met Jae and became the third member. Through her new team, she found a family she deeply values, even as her relationship with her biological family remains distant.

Personality

Karina is a cold and quiet individual, shaped by her struggles. Bunny’s influence helped her open up and rely more on her teammates. Now, as part of The Five, she prioritizes protecting her new family and avoiding further losses. Her reserved demeanor persists, but her actions convey her loyalty and determination.

Abilities

  • Superhuman speed and reflexes, enhanced by the Cheetah crystal
  • Dual knife combat expertise, blending precision and agility
  • Exceptional scouting and infiltration skills
