Origin

Overview

Squid is a quiet, loyal member of The Dominion, defined by secrecy. His name, origins, and true motives remain unknown. He follows The Lion's orders with unwavering discipline and is rumored to have served in an elite special ops unit.

Arc

Character Journey

Although his history is hidden, Squid's battlefield record is clear. His loyalty to The Dominion and The Lion defines him, and his combat performance has made him one of the most feared operatives in the conflict.

Combat Kit

Abilities

  • Two spear-like tentacles attached to chains wrapped around his arms
  • Expert close-quarters combat with sharp precision timing
  • Unpredictable long-range pressure through chained tentacle strikes
