Squid

[Unknown]

Overview

Squid is a quiet and loyal member of The Dominion, shrouded in mystery. No one knows his name, origins, or true motivations—he simply follows The Lion’s orders with unwavering dedication. Speculated to have been part of The Lion’s original special ops team, Squid is a dangerous and effective fighter.

Character Journey

While much of Squid’s past remains unknown, his actions on the battlefield speak for themselves. His loyalty to The Dominion and The Lion defines his identity, making him an enigmatic figure who commands fear and respect.

Abilities

  • Wields two spear-like tentacles attached to chains, wrapped around his arms
  • Expert in close-quarters combat with devastating precision
  • Unpredictable long-range attacks using his chain tentacles, making him a versatile and formidable opponent
