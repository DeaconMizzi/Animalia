Set-up

Animalia is an animated series of super powered individuals with animal-like powers and assets fighting for the sake of good or evil. The inspiration, in design at least, comes from the likes of Titanfall, Wild Kratts, and Power Rangers.

Each animal user is fused and powered by a crystal. Each crystal is specific to one species of an animal but the user can choose to represent it as a general choice (i.e Great White Shark vs Sharks). The rise in animal users causes the formation of several factions across the world.

The Animalian Research Corps (ARC) is one of the largest factions and is fighting for peace and stability in the world. With how powerful each user can be, they aim to keep balance in the world order. The Alliance was founded by the Gazelle, Elephant, and Rhino. Governments have entrusted the three to lead the organization, train up recruits, and keep disruptions at bay. This is one of the main groups we will follow.

The Dominion is led by the Lion and aims to create a new world order. The group is composed of mercenaries, thieves, soldiers, and rejects. Many have personal grievances against the ARC and its members while most just want to live in the new world order that the Lion promises will come to fruition.

Throughout this series we will see what makes each character tick, what their motivations are and how they handle and deal with the many aspects of life and the world. This will be relevant when we focus on a character and put them against society or their counterpart. Animalia aims to have great character moments as well as great action.

Themes

What makes Animalia stand out is its exploration of themes that are timeless and universally resonant. The series focuses on three key elements: identity, unity, and sacrifice.

As animalian users, the characters in Animalia know what it means to grapple with identity—how their new powers isolate them from their old lives, and how each struggles to find their place in the world. Like anyone who has ever felt like an outsider, our characters embody the conflicts of self-discovery. Yuna, the K-pop idol turned fugitive, loses everything she worked for but finds a deeper purpose in her new role. Matt, the college clarinet player, is thrust into a life of heroism and responsibility. Jaehyun, the anonymous vigilante, fights to protect his community while keeping his own identity hidden. Through these characters, audiences will recognize their own struggles with identity, belonging, and purpose.

Our series offers characters that reflect real-life personalities and experiences. The Five, as they train and grow, represent the journey of learning who they are, not just as individuals but as part of something greater. Characters like The Cheetah, a fierce yet reluctant leader, emphasize the complexities of friendship, loss, and personal growth. The journey toward embracing their true identities becomes a core driver of the series.

This leads directly to our second major theme: unity. Animalia is, at its heart, a story about coming together in the face of adversity. The Animalian Research Corps is not just a superhero group; it’s a family. The characters learn that their greatest strength lies not in their individual powers, but in their ability to work as a team, trust one another, and value their differences. The ideological conflict between The Dominion and The ARC explores how unity—or the lack of it—can shape the future of the world. By working together and embracing their diverse abilities, the characters demonstrate the power of cooperation and empathy, rising above their personal struggles to fight for a common cause.

Lastly, sacrifice is a theme that will define the journey of each character. Every hero in Animalia will face difficult choices, asking themselves how far they are willing to go for what they believe in. Whether it’s Yuna giving up her stardom, or Cheetah mourning the loss of Bunny, the characters are constantly confronted with the question of what they’re willing to sacrifice for the greater good. The ARC, facing impossible odds in the season finale as the Leviathan is attacked, underscores that protecting what you love often comes with heartbreaking losses.

In Animalia, growth is essential. Characters must evolve, not just in terms of their powers but in their understanding of what it means to carry the weight of responsibility. The series will highlight that true strength doesn’t come from powers alone, but from the choices the characters make, the sacrifices they endure, and the unity they build. As they evolve, they mature, learn, and discover that being a hero is not about the abilities they possess but the decisions they make in the face of adversity.

The World/Lore

Extremely powerful crystals were brought into the world eras ago by someone or something unknown. A creation of a higher being? Remnants of a prehistoric civilization? An evolutionary anomaly? While the history of the crystals themselves is not well known to anyone, one thing is certain, they’ve been all over the Earth for a long time laying dormant. Only recently have they woken up and have started to reappear across the world. This emergence has led to some people gaining powers associated with these crystals. Some choose to devote much of their time to seeking out these crystals, while others seemingly have the crystal appear to them. To the public eye, these newly powered individuals are superheroes, villains, and vigilantes that are now a part of society. A few years after the emergence, crystals have been widely accepted as an inescapable part of life. Average civilians often do not encounter crystal-powered individuals, but the instances when they do often cause much publicity. This has led many people to form their own opinions about the factions who use the crystals, with some believing that they are putting the world at risk while others believe that the guarding of the crystals is necessary from letting them fall into the hands of evil.

Crystals are a small piece of mineral that has a blue shine and has an animal silhouette embedded into it. They can be found wherever in the world, underground, in ancient structures, or rarely just appearing in front of people. When a crystal bonds with a person, the process can vary between pain depending on the power of the animal and user tolerance. The bonding process merges smoothly with its user; the crystal turning from physical to more mystical as it enters its host. Visually, the process is similar to Rourke’s transformation (Atlantis, The Lost Empire), but not life threatening. The crystal grants the user feats of strength, giving general superhuman abilities, but certain aspects would be more enhanced than others based on the type of animal once received (i.e Cheetah has enhanced agility). Small perk, the crystal can copy the armor of the user so for convenience they can activate their armor at will, the armor would need to be made first.

Crystals can be extracted one of two ways. An extractor was made by The Dominion to extract a crystal out of an individual, in a not so pretty way. Crystal can reform again after death however they are launched away from where they were, essentially needing to hunt after that crystal again somewhere in the world.
